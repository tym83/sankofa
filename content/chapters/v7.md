Afrofuturism looks forward by looking back. [[sun-ra]], from Birmingham, Alabama, said he had been carried to Saturn. [[octavia-butler]] became the first science fiction writer to win a MacArthur grant.

On 11 August 1973 [[cindy-campbell]] threw a party in a Bronx rec room to raise money for back-to-school clothes, and her brother Clive played the records. That night is commonly called the birthday of [[hip-hop]]. Hear what came next in the [[amen-break]]. [[planet-rock]] set Kraftwerk's melody to a Japanese drum machine. In [[detroit-techno]], Juan Atkins linked his terms to Alvin Toffler's techno-rebels, and a 1988 compilation helped fix the name; the music found a second home in Berlin. [[chicago-house]] took its name from the Warehouse club. In London, [[rinse-fm]] started in a council-flat kitchen and helped make grime. The culture also argues with itself: see [[misogyny-in-hip-hop]].

The future was built in hardware and in letters, too. [[jerry-lawson]] led the team behind swappable game cartridges. Two brothers invented [[adlam]] for the Fulani language. And in 1992 Nnebue's Living in Bondage became a breakthrough video hit, a landmark for [[nollywood]].

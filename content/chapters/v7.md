Sankofa means going back to fetch what was left behind, so that you can go forward. [[sun-ra]], from Birmingham, Alabama, said he had been carried to Saturn and told to speak through music. [[octavia-butler]] became the first science fiction writer to win a MacArthur grant.

On 11 August 1973 a girl in the Bronx needed money for back-to-school clothes. [[cindy-campbell]] threw a party in a Bronx rec room and asked her brother Clive to play the records. That night is commonly called the birthday of [[hip-hop]], a way to stretch a few seconds of funk drums into minutes. Its building blocks are samples, like the six seconds of the [[amen-break]], whose drummer saw no royalties. Container images are built the same way, layer on layer.

Influence makes circles. [[planet-rock]] set Kraftwerk's melody to a Japanese drum machine. [[detroit-techno]] took its name from a futurist's book and found a second home in Berlin. [[chicago-house]] took its name from a Chicago club, the Warehouse.

The culture has its own critics, and they are part of it: [[misogyny-in-hip-hop]] is their story. In London, [[rinse-fm]] started in a council-flat kitchen and helped make grime.

The future is also built. [[jerry-lawson]] led the team behind swappable game cartridges. Two brothers in Guinea invented [[adlam]] for the Fulani language, and now it ships in Windows. In Lagos, the story goes, a trader whose imported VHS tapes stopped selling put a film on them, and [[nollywood]] had its first hit. The dictionary does not end here: every card leads to another.

Ngũgĩ wa Thiong'o wrote his first Gikuyu novel on prison toilet paper. In 1977 peasants and workers at the Kamiriithu cultural centre near Limuru staged Ngaahika Ndeenda, "I Will Marry When I Want", a Gikuyu play he co-wrote. That December he was arrested and held without charge until December 1978. In prison he wrote Caitaani Mũtharaba-Inĩ, Devil on the Cross. Police later razed the theatre. Decolonising the Mind (1986), he wrote, was his farewell to English. He died in the United States in May 2025. On writing your own language, see [[nko]] and [[adlam]]; compare [[achebe]].

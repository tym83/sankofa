In 1977 peasants and workers at the Kamiriithu cultural centre near
Limuru staged Ngaahika Ndeenda, "I Will Marry When I Want", a Gikuyu play
Ngũgĩ co-wrote about who gets rich in independent Kenya. That December he was arrested and held
without charge in Kamiti Maximum Security Prison until December 1978. There,
on prison toilet paper, he wrote Caitaani Mũtharaba-Inĩ, Devil on the Cross.
Police later razed the theatre. Decolonising the Mind (1986), he
wrote, was his farewell to English. He died in the United States in May
2025. On writing your own language, see [[nko]] and [[adlam]].

From around 1600, people who fled the sugar plantations of Pernambuco built a
network of villages in the palm forests of what is now Alagoas. Its heart
was the Serra da Barriga. Palmares, also remembered as Angola Janga, beat
back Dutch and Portuguese expeditions for decades under Ganga Zumba and then
his nephew Zumbi. In February 1694 the deadliest expedition, led by
bandeirantes hired from São Paulo, took about 500 prisoners to Recife. Zumbi escaped, but was killed
on 20 November 1695 and his head sent to Recife. Brazil's Black movement
made that date its own; law 14.759 of December 2023 made it a national
holiday, the Dia de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra. Compare
[[nanny-of-the-maroons|Jamaica's maroons]] and [[capoeira]].

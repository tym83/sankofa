In 1965 James Brown cut "Papa's Got a Brand New Bag" in under an hour on
the way to a show, and, in Rolling Stone's words, "all of a sudden, there
was funk". With "Cold Sweat" (1967) the song nearly dissolved into
interlocking rhythm, with a drum break by Clyde Stubblefield. His
short solo on "Funky Drummer" (1970) has been sampled on hundreds of
songs, including Public Enemy's "Fight the Power" (1989); he said he was never
credited or paid. In 1976 George Clinton's Parliament-Funkadelic landed a
Mothership on stage: Black "afronauts", Afrofuturism in the spirit of
[[sun-ra]]. A
replica now stands in the Smithsonian. Funk fed [[hip-hop]] and, with
highlife, [[afrobeat]].

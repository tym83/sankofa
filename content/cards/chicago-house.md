By Frankie Knuckles's own account, the genre was named after his club: he saw "house music" on a shop sign, and a friend explained it meant what he played at the Warehouse. Others also claim the name. In 1977 Knuckles moved from New York into the Warehouse, at 206 South Jefferson Street, where young, mostly Black and gay men danced. On 12 July 1979 radio DJ Steve Dahl blew up disco records at Comiskey Park; whether Disco Demolition was racist and homophobic is still argued, though Dahl denies it. Chicago kept dancing, and in 2004 it named a stretch of Jefferson Street Frankie Knuckles Way. Cousins: [[detroit-techno]], [[planet-rock]], [[funk]].

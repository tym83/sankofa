Techno's story runs from a small-town Michigan high school to a Berlin club. Juan Atkins, Derrick May and Kevin Saunderson met at high school in Belleville, outside Detroit, and became the Belleville Three. Atkins worked with Richard Davis as Cybotron, whose "Clear" is usually dated 1982, and ran his own label, Metroplex. He said he took the word techno from Alvin Toffler's The Third Wave. In 1991, in a Berlin reinventing itself after the Wall, Underground Resistance's "X-101" was the first release on the new Tresor label. Atkins died in 2026.

Juan Atkins, Derrick May and Kevin Saunderson met at high school in
Belleville, a small town outside Detroit, and became the Belleville Three.
Around 1979 Atkins was recording at home with two cassette decks and a Korg
synthesizer, making a collage of Funkadelic and Kraftwerk. With Richard
Davis, as Cybotron, he released "Clear" (1982), an almost wordless machine
track that spent nine weeks on the Black music chart. He took the word
techno from Alvin Toffler's The Third Wave. His label Metroplex put out May's
and Saunderson's first records in 1986. In 1991, in a Berlin reinventing
itself after the Wall, Underground Resistance's "X-101" was the first
release on the new Tresor label. Atkins died in 2026. Cousins:
[[planet-rock]], [[chicago-house]], [[funk]], [[sun-ra]].

In 1789 a former sailor and campaigner in London published his own life story,
paid for in advance by subscribers. The list opens with the Prince of Wales;
[[granville-sharp]] took two copies. *The Interesting Narrative* of Olaudah
Equiano, also called Gustavus Vassa, became a bestseller and ran to nine
editions by 1794. It tells of a childhood in Igbo country, kidnapping, the
Middle Passage, and the day in 1766 when he bought his own freedom for £40. In
March 1783 he brought news of the [[zong]] killings to Sharp. Then the twist:
Vincent Carretta found a 1759 baptism record and a 1773 Navy muster roll that
both give his birthplace as Carolina. Whether the African chapters are memory or
gathered testimony is still contested.

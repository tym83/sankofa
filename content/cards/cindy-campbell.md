On 11 August 1973 Cindy Campbell, still in high school, rented the
recreation room of her family's building at 1520 Sedgwick Avenue in the
Bronx for $25. She needed new clothes for the school year. She wrote
invitations on index cards, charged 25 cents for girls and 50 for boys, and
asked her 18-year-old brother Clive to play the music. He became DJ Kool
Herc; she went home with a few hundred dollars. The night is now commonly
called the birth of [[hip-hop]], though no genre starts at one party. Both
siblings were born in Kingston and grew up inside its sound-system culture
([[reggae]]). The party that is remembered as the start was a young woman's idea; compare
[[misogyny-in-hip-hop]].

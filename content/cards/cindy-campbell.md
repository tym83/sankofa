Hip-hop's famous birthday party was a high-school girl's fundraiser. On 11 August 1973 Cindy Campbell held a party in the recreation room of 1520 Sedgwick Avenue in the Bronx because she needed money for new clothes for the school year. She wrote the invitations on index cards and asked her brother Clive to play the music. He became DJ Kool Herc, born in Kingston and raised inside its sound-system culture ([[reggae]]). The night is commonly called the birth of [[hip-hop]], though no genre starts at one party. The start remembered was a young woman's idea; compare [[misogyny-in-hip-hop]].

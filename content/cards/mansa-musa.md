Mansa Musa brought so much gold through Cairo that it flooded the market and depressed its value, as the writer al-Umari recorded. In 1324 Musa, ruler of Mali, stopped in Cairo on his way to Mecca. Half a century later a Majorcan mapmaker painted "Musse Melly" on the Catalan Atlas: crowned, holding a gold disc, the richest king of the region. Mali's founding hero is [[sunjata|Sunjata Keita, still sung in the epic that bears his name]]; its scholarship lives on in the [[timbuktu-manuscripts|Timbuktu manuscripts]]. Compare the court art of the [[benin-bronzes|Benin Bronzes]].

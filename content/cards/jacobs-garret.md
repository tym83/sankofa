For nearly seven years Harriet Jacobs lived in a space too low to stand in, watching her children through a hole. In 1835, escaping Dr. James Norcom and his years of sexual threats, she hid in a crawlspace at her grandmother's house in Edenton, North Carolina. "The garret was only nine feet long, and seven wide. The highest part was three feet high," she wrote. From there she watched her children in the street. In 1842 she escaped by boat to the North. In 1861 she published Incidents in the Life of a Slave Girl under the name Linda Brent.

The violin sonata everyone knows as the "Kreutzer" was written for George
Bridgetower, a virtuoso of African descent. In May 1803 he and Beethoven
premiered it in Vienna, Bridgetower reading parts of it almost at sight.
Beethoven's sketch carried a joking dedication to him. Then the two men
quarrelled, reportedly over a woman, and Beethoven gave the dedication to
Rodolphe Kreutzer, who never played it.

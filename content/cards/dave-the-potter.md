"I wonder where is all my relation / Friendship to all—and every nation": an enslaved man wrote that on a stoneware jar on 16 August 1857. Dave, born into slavery around 1800, turned huge alkaline-glazed storage jars in the potteries of Edgefield, South Carolina. In 1834 he signed and dated a jar, and he kept writing for decades: his name, dates and couplets. Freed in the 1860s, he became David Drake. In 2025 the MFA Boston returned two of his jars to his descendants. Compare [[ashleys-sack|Ashley's Sack]], another object that speaks for a family, and [[phillis-wheatley|Phillis Wheatley]].

Jamaica's first and only woman National Hero led guerrillas in the hills. Nanny led the Windward Maroons of the Blue Mountains against the British, fighting with ambushes in the hills. Maroon memory adds legends, such as Nanny catching bullets, that no document supports. When Captain Quao signed a peace treaty in 1739, she reportedly opposed it. What is on paper: a land patent of 1740, still in the Jamaica Archives, granting land to "Nanny" and her people, 500 or about 1,000 acres by differing accounts. Jamaica made her a National Hero, in a year given as 1975, 1976 or 1982, and she is on the 500-dollar note. Compare [[palmares]] and [[yaa-asantewaa]].

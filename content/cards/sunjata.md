Sunjata Keita founded the Mali Empire in the early thirteenth century, after defeating Sumaoro Kanté, king of Sosso, and he has never stopped being sung. His story lives as performance, not as a book: the jeli, or griot, trained from childhood, sings the epic to the balafon. Sankofa's Griot operator is named for these keepers of memory. See [[mansa-musa|Mansa Musa]], [[talking-drums|Talking drums]] and [[timbuktu-manuscripts|the Timbuktu manuscripts]].

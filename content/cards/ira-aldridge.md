Taras Shevchenko, a poet born a serf, watched Ira Aldridge play Othello in St Petersburg, became his friend and drew his portrait. Barred from a real career in New York, where he had acted with the African Grove company, Aldridge sailed for Britain at seventeen. In October 1825 he made his London debut at the Royal Coburg, now the Old Vic, as Oroonoko in The Revolt of Surinam. He became famous as Othello and Aaron the Moor, then toured Europe, playing in English to casts speaking German or Swedish. The St Petersburg Othello came in November 1858; the drawing is dated 25 December. He died on tour in Łódź in 1867. Even his birth year is disputed: 1805 or 1807.

Barred from a real career in New York, where he had acted with the African
Grove company, Aldridge sailed for Britain at seventeen. In October 1825 he
made his London debut at the Royal Coburg, now the Old Vic, as Oroonoko in
The Revolt of Surinam. He became famous as Othello and Aaron the Moor, then
toured Europe, where he played in English to casts speaking German or
Swedish. On 10 November 1858 he opened as Othello in St Petersburg; in the
audience sat Taras Shevchenko, a poet born a serf. They became friends, and
on 25 December Shevchenko drew him, Aldridge singing and dancing between
poses. He died on tour in Łódź in 1867. Compare [[bridgetower]] and
[[gannibal]].

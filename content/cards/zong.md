In late 1781 the Liverpool-owned slave ship *Zong*, packed with about 470
captives, overshot Jamaica. Between 29 November and 1 December the crew threw
more than 130 Africans into the sea. Back in Britain the owners claimed them on
insurance, and in March 1783 a jury at London's Guildhall found for the owners.
On 19 March [[equiano]] carried the news to [[granville-sharp]], who demanded a
murder trial and never got one. In May Lord Mansfield's court granted the
insurers a new trial; there is no evidence it was ever held. Sharp had that
hearing taken down, and the record is most of what survives. In 2008 M.
NourbeSe Philip built a book-length poem, *Zong!*, only from the words of the
case report, Gregson v. Gilbert.

On 16 March 1827 two free Black men, Samuel Cornish and John Russwurm,
launched *Freedom's Journal*, the first African American owned and operated
newspaper in the United States. Its first front page declared: "We wish to
plead our own cause. Too long have others spoken for us." The weekly carried
news, biographies, births and deaths, and attacks on slavery and
discrimination. Then it split over Africa. Cornish left in September 1827 as
Russwurm, among the first Black college graduates in America (Bowdoin, 1826), moved
towards the American Colonization Society. When he declared for colonization
in 1829, readers abandoned the paper; the last issue is dated 28 March 1829.
By the end of the year Russwurm was in [[liberia]], where he later governed
Maryland-in-Liberia. Heirs: [[ida-b-wells]], [[chicago-defender]].

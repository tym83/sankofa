Herman Poole Blount was born in Birmingham, Alabama, in 1914 and later
denied both the birth and the name. As a student at Alabama A&M, he said,
he was carried to Saturn and told he "would speak, and the world would
listen". In Chicago he
became Le Sony'r Ra, legally, in 1952, built the Arkestra in the 1950s and,
with Alton Abraham, ran El Saturn Records, one of the first labels owned by
a Black musician. From 1968 the band lived in a Philadelphia house and pressed records
with hand-drawn covers. In 1972
they shot the film Space Is the Place. In the early 1990s the critic Mark
Dery gave the idea a name, Afrofuturism. Its heirs include
[[octavia-butler]], [[planet-rock]] and [[detroit-techno]]; its roots are
in [[jazz]].

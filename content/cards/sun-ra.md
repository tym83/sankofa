Herman Poole Blount was born in Birmingham, Alabama, in 1914, and later denied both the birth and the name. In Chicago he legally became Le Sony'r Ra in 1952. There he built the Arkestra in the 1950s and ran his own label, El Saturn Records. From 1968 the band lived together in a Philadelphia house. In the early 1990s the critic Mark Dery gave the idea a name, Afrofuturism. Its heirs include [[octavia-butler|Octavia E. Butler]], [[planet-rock|"Planet Rock"]] and [[detroit-techno|Detroit techno]]; its roots are in [[jazz|jazz]].

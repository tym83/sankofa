A German band's music, a Japanese drum machine, and a dollar a record for
Kraftwerk: that was "Planet Rock". In 1982 Afrika Bambaataa cut it with
producer Arthur Baker for Tom Silverman's new label, Tommy Boy. Bambaataa
already played Kraftwerk's "Trans-Europe Express" in his sets, and the beat
of "Numbers" went in too; they rebuilt both on a Roland TR-808. Kraftwerk
were angry, and Tommy Boy paid them for every copy. From 2016 several men
accused Bambaataa of abusing them as boys; he denied it and was never
criminally charged, and the accusations remain contested. In 2025 he lost a
civil suit by default. He died in 2026. See [[hip-hop]], [[funk]],
[[detroit-techno]] and [[chicago-house]].

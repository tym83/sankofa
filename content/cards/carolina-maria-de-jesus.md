Carolina Maria de Jesus, from Sacramento in Minas Gerais, had two years of
school. In São Paulo she built a shack in the Canindé favela, raised three
children and walked the streets, her son on her back, selling scrap paper to
junkyards. She wrote poems and a diary in notebooks and on paper that
came out of the trash. In April 1958 the reporter Audálio Dantas saw them
and printed excerpts; in 1960 Quarto de Despejo, "Room of Garbage", sold out
its first edition in a week, more than 10,000 copies, and passed 90,000 in six months.
How much Dantas edited it is contested. As Child of the Dark it reached
readers in English. Her later books met indifference; she died in Parelheiros in 1977. Compare
[[what-black-means]] and [[hurston]].

Carolina Maria de Jesus had two years of school and a living made from collecting waste paper, and she wrote. Born in Sacramento, Minas Gerais, she built a shack in the Canindé favela of São Paulo and raised three children there. In 1958 the reporter Audálio Dantas saw her notebooks and printed excerpts. In 1960 the diary came out as Quarto de Despejo, "Room of Garbage", and as Child of the Dark it reached readers in English. How much Dantas edited it is contested.

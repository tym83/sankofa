Benin's artists made the Portuguese their subject. The Oba's brass casters, the royal guild of Igun Eronmwon, put the Portuguese newcomers onto the palace plaques as foreign merchants. They worked in lost wax: a detailed wax model over a clay core, clay over the wax, the wax melted out and molten metal poured in. In 1897 a British force took Benin City and carried thousands of objects off to Europe. Restitution has begun: in 2022 London's Horniman Museum transferred ownership of 72 objects to Nigeria, and in December 2022 Germany's foreign minister handed 20 back in Abuja. Their kin in craft: [[kente|kente cloth]], [[adinkra|adinkra signs]] and [[nsibidi|Nsibidi]].

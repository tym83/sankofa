The man called the father of gospel music first made his name in the
[[blues]]. Thomas A. Dorsey came to Chicago from Georgia in 1916, and as
"Georgia Tom" he led Ma Rainey's band. Then he began setting sacred words
to blues patterns. In 1932 his wife Nettie died in childbirth, and their
son died the next day. In his grief he wrote "Take My Hand, Precious
Lord". In 1933 he co-founded a national convention of gospel choirs, and
later teamed with Mahalia Jackson, who had come up from New Orleans. Hear
where it came from in [[spirituals]], and where it went with
[[rosetta-tharpe|Rosetta Tharpe]] and [[funk]].

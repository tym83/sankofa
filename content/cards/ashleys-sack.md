A cotton sack holds a whole family's memory. When nine-year-old Ashley was sold in South Carolina, her mother Rose gave her a sack with a tattered dress, three handfuls of pecans and a braid of Rose's hair. Generations later, Ashley's granddaughter embroidered that story onto the sack and signed it "Ruth Middleton 1921". Mark Auslander traced a Rose and an Ashley to the Charleston estate of Robert Martin, who died in 1852, a reconstruction rather than proof. The sack has been shown at Middleton Place and at the Smithsonian's African American history museum.

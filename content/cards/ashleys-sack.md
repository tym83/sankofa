A patched cotton sack surfaced at a Tennessee flea market in 2007. Its lower third carries ten embroidered lines: "My great
grandmother Rose mother of Ashley gave her this sack when she was sold at age 9
in South Carolina it held a tattered dress 3 handfulls of pecans a braid of
Roses hair. Told her It be filled with my Love always she never saw her again
Ashley is my grandmother Ruth Middleton 1921". Historian Mark Auslander traced
a Rose and an Ashley to the Charleston estate of Robert Martin, who died in
1852. The sack went on display at Middleton Place and the Smithsonian's
African American history museum, and Tiya Miles's book about it won the 2021
National Book Award. Compare [[dave-the-potter]] and [[partus-1662]].

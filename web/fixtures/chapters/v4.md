The African diaspora reached places no map of the slave trade shows. A boy taken
from Africa became the godson of Peter the Great: [[abram-gannibal]], engineer,
general, and great-grandfather of Pushkin.

In Paris, a planter's son from Guadeloupe became the best swordsman in France
and one of its finest violinists. The [[chevalier-de-saint-georges]] conducted
orchestras that commissioned Haydn.

An actor from New York, [[ira-aldridge]], could not play Shakespeare at home. He
played him across Europe instead, from London to Saint Petersburg, where a
Ukrainian poet drew his portrait.

In Brazil, the largest destination of the slave trade, the enslaved turned a
fight into a game. [[capoeira]] was banned, hidden and taught, and is now played
around the world.

On 13 May 1888 Brazil became the last country in the Americas to end slavery.
The [[lei-aurea|Golden Law]] has two articles; the movement that won it had
thousands of people, from the lawyer Luiz Gama to the raft fishermen of
Fortaleza who refused to row captives to the ships.

The word sankofa means: go back and fetch it. The last chapter looks forward by
looking back. [[sun-ra]] said he came from Saturn, and in Chicago he built an
orchestra to prove it.

Critics later gave the long tradition a name. [[afrofuturism]] imagines Black futures,
from spaceships on funk stages to Africa as a source of technology.

In Detroit, a city the car plants left behind, three friends made machine music
for the future. [[detroit-techno]] crossed the ocean to a reunited Berlin.

Two of its producers, [[drexciya]], told the [[middle-passage]] again as a
story of survival: a nation under the sea.

And [[octavia-butler]], who wrote of a woman pulled back into slavery and of a
California falling apart, has her name on the surface of Mars. The ships that
started this story have become spacecraft, and the
museum ends where it began, with a story fetched back and carried forward.

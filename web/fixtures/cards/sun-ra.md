Herman Poole Blount of Birmingham, Alabama, said that as a young man he had been
taken to Saturn and told to speak to the world through music. In Chicago in the
1950s he renamed himself Sun Ra and formed the Arkestra, which wore robes and
headdresses, played electric keyboards before almost anyone else, and lived
together in a house in Philadelphia from 1968. With his partner Alton Abraham he
released records on their own label, El Saturn, often in sleeves painted by
hand. His film Space Is the Place offered Black people a planet of their own. He
is the elder of [[afrofuturism]], born out of [[jazz]].

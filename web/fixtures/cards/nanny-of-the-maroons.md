In the Blue Mountains of eastern Jamaica, the Windward Maroons, people who had
escaped plantations, fought British troops through the 1720s and 1730s.
Tradition remembers their leader Nanny as a strategist and spiritual guide who
directed ambushes from Nanny Town on a ridge above the Rio Grande. British
documents barely mention her; one is a land patent of 1740 granting five hundred
acres to "Nanny and the people now residing with her". Her people still live
in Moore Town. Legends say she could catch bullets; historians treat these as
legend, and Jamaica named her a National Hero in 1975. Compare [[palmares]] in
Brazil.

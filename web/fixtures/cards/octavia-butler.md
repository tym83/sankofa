A shy, tall girl from Pasadena who began writing stories as a child, Butler sold
her first stories in the early 1970s and worked night jobs for years. In Kindred
(1979) a Black woman in 1976 California is pulled back to a Maryland plantation
of the 1810s, the past of [[douglass|Frederick Douglass]] and
[[jacobs|Harriet Jacobs]]. Parable of the Sower (1993) imagines California in
the 2020s, broken by climate and greed. In 1995 she became the first science
fiction writer to win a MacArthur fellowship. Her papers are at the Huntington
Library, and her name is on the surface of Mars. She is the novelist of
[[afrofuturism]].

He began at the African Grove Theatre in New York, a company run by Black
actors, and left for England at seventeen because American stages were closed to
him. In 1833 he played Othello at Covent Garden. Over the next three decades he
toured Europe as Othello, Shylock, Macbeth and even King Lear, and in the 1850s
and 1860s he took Shakespeare across the Russian Empire, playing in English with
local casts. In Saint Petersburg in 1858 he befriended the Ukrainian poet Taras
Shevchenko, a former serf, who sketched him. Aldridge died on tour in Łódź in
1867 and is buried there. Compare [[chevalier-de-saint-georges]].

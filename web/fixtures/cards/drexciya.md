James Stinson and Gerald Donald released hard, fast electro records from 1992
and never showed their faces. Track titles and sleeves built a mythology. The
liner notes of The Quest (1997) ask whether the unborn children of pregnant
women thrown from ships in the [[middle-passage]] could have learned to breathe
water and founded a nation, Drexciya, at the bottom of the Atlantic. In a few
lines they turned the grief of the [[zong]] into a science fiction of survival.
The project ended with Stinson's death in 2002, and writers and musicians keep
returning to the story. It is the deep end of [[detroit-techno]].

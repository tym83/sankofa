In 1999 a book told the story of Ozella McDaniel Williams, a Charleston quilt
seller, who said her family had passed down a code: patterns such as Monkey
Wrench and Wagon Wheel, hung on fences, told fugitives on the
[[underground-railroad]] when and where to run. The idea spread into schools
and museum shops. Quilt historians found no trace of it in diaries, memoirs or
the [[wpa-narratives]], and the dates of several patterns do not fit. So this
card is a myth. The real wonder is the quilts themselves, such as Harriet
Powers's Bible Quilt of 1886, which tells scripture in appliqué.

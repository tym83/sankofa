She learned guitar in the Church of God in Christ and in 1938 took gospel into
the Cotton Club and onto Decca records, singing about the Lord over an amplified
guitar full of bends and blues runs. Little Richard and Johnny Cash
named her as an influence; she is an undeclared dependency of rock and roll. In
July 1951 she married at Griffith Stadium in Washington before a paying crowd.
In 1964 she played on a disused railway platform in Manchester, in the rain, for
television, and young British musicians watched. She joined the Rock & Roll
Hall of Fame only in 2018. Her roots are in the [[spirituals]] and the
[[blues]].

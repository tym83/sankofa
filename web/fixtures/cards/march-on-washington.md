On 28 August 1963 about 250,000 people filled the Mall from the Washington
Monument to the Lincoln Memorial, demanding jobs and freedom. The march was the
idea of A. Philip Randolph, the union leader of the Pullman porters, and it was
organised in eight weeks by Bayard Rustin. Marian Anderson and Mahalia Jackson
sang. Late in the afternoon Martin Luther King Jr. was reading his text when,
by several accounts, Jackson called out "Tell them about the dream, Martin", and
he set the page aside. Eight years earlier [[rosa-parks]] had started the
boycott that made him a leader. [[nina-simone]] was about to write her answer.

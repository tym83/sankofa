Sailor, merchant, hairdresser, Arctic explorer, campaigner: Equiano bought his
own freedom in 1766 and settled in London. In 1789 he published The Interesting
Narrative, which describes his childhood in Igboland, his kidnapping and the
crossing of the [[middle-passage]]. He sold it himself and toured with it until
it had gone through nine editions. In 1783 he brought news of the [[zong]]
massacre to the abolitionist Granville Sharp. Two documents found by the
historian Vincent Carretta give South Carolina as his birthplace, so whether he
remembered the crossing or gathered it from others is disputed. The book's
power to move a nation is not.

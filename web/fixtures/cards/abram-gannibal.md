Taken from Africa as a boy and brought to Constantinople, he was sent to Russia
around 1704 and became the godson and valet of Peter the Great. Peter sent him
to France, where he studied military engineering at La Fère and fought in the
French army. Back in Russia he built fortresses, survived exile to Siberia after
Peter's death, and rose to general-in-chief, owning estates near Pskov. His
great-grandson Alexander Pushkin was proud of his "African blood" and in 1827
began a novel, The Moor of Peter the Great, which he never finished. Where
Gannibal was born is still argued about.

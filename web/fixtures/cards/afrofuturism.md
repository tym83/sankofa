The critic Mark Dery named it in 1993, asking why so few Black writers wrote
science fiction when their ancestors had been abducted by strangers in ships.
The practice was older. [[sun-ra]] said he came from Saturn; Parliament landed a
Mothership on stage; [[octavia-butler]] wrote time travel back into slavery.
Detroit producers made [[detroit-techno]] and [[drexciya]] invented a sea
people. Afrofuturism looks back to look forward, which is what sankofa means:
go back and fetch it. It treats the [[middle-passage]] as the first alien
abduction and Africa as a source of future technology.

Three friends from Belleville, a small town outside Detroit, Juan Atkins,
Derrick May and Kevin Saunderson, listened to Kraftwerk, Parliament and the
radio DJ The Electrifying Mojo, and made dance music with cheap drum machines in
a city emptied by the decline of the car plants. Atkins had read Alvin Toffler
on the coming information age. A British compilation in 1988 made the sound's
name, techno, stick. After the Berlin Wall fell, Detroit artists played in the new clubs
of East Berlin, and the music became the soundtrack of a reunited city. It
belongs to [[afrofuturism]] and gave birth to [[drexciya]].

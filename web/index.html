Sankofa

Seven centuries of Black culture, from the libraries of Timbuktu to the starships of Sun Ra.

Sankofa is an Adinkra symbol of the Akan people of Ghana: a bird that turns its head back to take an egg from its own back. Go back and fetch what you left behind.

Descend through seven centuries Listen to the cluster Or begin before the ships

In 2020, Kubernetes began to retire a word. The machine that runs the cluster stopped being called the master and became the control plane. Most engineers changed a label and moved on. This museum starts at that commit and goes back: to where the word came from, and to everything built, sung, written and invented by the people that pair of words was once made of.

It is a distribution and a monument at once. Every era is a release, from v0 to v7: Africa before the slave trade, the Middle Passage, the revolutions, the voices, the diaspora, Harlem and Négritude, civil rights, Afrofuturism. Every page is a card: a few lines, one surprise, and a road further.

Cards come from three books. The Book of Voices ❝ is told by people in their own words. The Book of Archives ▤ is the ledger kept by those who traded and ruled. The Book of Heirs ✶ is the art and argument of the diaspora. As in Pavić’s Dictionary of the Khazars, the books do not always agree, and the museum exists in two copies, male and female.
